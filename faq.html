Boiler questions Burnaby homeowners ask

These are the questions Google shows people who search for boiler repair in Burnaby. The answers below are plain and short. None of them quote a price, because a price that is not based on your house is not worth anything.

What are the most common boiler problems?

The faults a technician sees most often are a loss of system pressure, a failure to ignite or hold a flame, no heat in some radiators while others run hot, new noise from the unit, and a leak at a fitting or at the pressure relief valve.

Nearly all of those are symptoms, not causes. Low pressure, for example, means water is leaving the system somewhere — the gauge tells you that it happens, not where. So the first visit is a diagnosis.

What is the average lifespan of a boiler?

An average is the least useful number here. Lifespan turns on three things: the unit itself, the quality of the water it has been running on, and whether anyone serviced it each year.

A boiler that got an annual service and runs on clean system water lasts a great deal longer than an identical one that got neither. If you want a real answer for your own unit, that comes from an inspection — the model, the heat exchanger condition and the service history together.

Is it worth replacing a 20 year old boiler?

Age on its own does not settle it. Four things do:

  • Are replacement parts still made for it?
  • How does its efficiency compare with a current unit, across a full Burnaby heating season?
  • Is the heat exchanger sound?
  • What does this repair cost against a replacement — and is it the first repair or the third?

If parts are discontinued or the heat exchanger has gone, replacement is usually the answer. If it is a controls fault on an otherwise sound unit, repair usually is. See boiler service and installation for what a replacement involves.

How much does a new boiler cost in Canada?

There is no single figure, and any page that gives you one without seeing the property is guessing. What sets the price is the heat output your home actually needs, the type of boiler, whether the existing venting and gas line suit the new unit, and how much of the current system can be reused.

Two houses on the same Burnaby street can differ by a wide margin on the venting alone. Ask for a written quote against your own house, and ask what is included in it.

How much should it cost to fix a boiler?

A repair is normally a service call, then parts and labour. Nobody can price the parts before the fault is found, so the diagnosis comes first and the repair price after it.

Two questions worth asking when you book: is the service call credited against the repair if you go ahead, and is there a callback charge if the same fault returns.
