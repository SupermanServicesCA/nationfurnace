Heating and cooling in Burnaby since 2003

We install and repair furnaces, boilers, heat pumps, air conditioning and hot water systems in houses and commercial buildings across Burnaby.

Call (604) 299-5006

Dino Gerami started the company in Burnaby in 2003, and we've worked out of the same city ever since.

Burnaby housing isn't one thing. There are post-war houses where the heating has been rebuilt two or three times over, and there are towers going up around Metrotown and Brentwood that were designed around the equipment we fit new. A furnace swap in a 1950s basement usually means working around whatever ductwork the last few owners left behind. That's a different afternoon than a heat pump on a new build.

What we work on

Heating, cooling, hot water and ventilation, as service calls, repairs or full installs. The breakdown is on what we do.

Commercial work too

Rooftop units, make-up air, and the heating plant in strata and light industrial buildings. Still Creek and the Lake City industrial area are close by, so a good share of the commercial work is within a few minutes of the shop.
