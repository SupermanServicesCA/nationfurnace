Where we work

We run out of Still Creek Drive and cover Burnaby north and south, plus the neighbouring parts of Vancouver.

The parts of the city are not the same job. Older stock keeps us on radiators and heating that has been rebuilt more than once. Around Metrotown and Brentwood there's a lot of newer building with equipment we fit from scratch. Both keep us busy, in different months.

When to book

Winter fills up with heating calls and the cooling calls all land in the same week the first real heat does. If what you need is a service rather than a repair, booking it outside those two stretches gets you a better appointment than waiting for something to fail in January.
