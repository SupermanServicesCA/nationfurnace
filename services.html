What we do

Heating, cooling, hot water and ventilation. Any of it as a service call, a repair, or a full replacement.

Furnaces

The bulk of our winter. Ignition faults, blower motors, cracked heat exchangers, and the annual service that catches most of it before December. When a furnace is past saving we handle the swap, including whatever the old ductwork needs to make the new unit breathe properly.

We install Ducane, Coleman and Lennox equipment, among others. More on furnace work.

Boilers

Hydronic systems, radiators and in-floor heat. Pressure loss, circulation pumps, controls that have drifted, and full replacements when a unit is finished. A good share of Burnaby heats with hot water rather than forced air, so there's more on boiler work here.

Heat pumps

One unit that heats in winter and cools in summer, which is why they have taken off here. We install and service inverter mini-splits and ducted systems, and we'll tell you honestly whether your house suits one before you spend the money.

Air conditioning

Installs, seasonal start-ups and repairs. Try to book the service before the first real heat arrives rather than during it. The whole trade gets buried in the same week. See air conditioning.

Gas fireplaces

Pilot and ignition faults, glass, log sets, and new inserts. Run a fireplace that has sat all summer once in the fall, before you need it. Better to find a dead pilot in October than in a cold snap.

Hot water

Tanks and tankless both. Tanks are usually thermostats, elements, sediment, or a leak that has been ignored for a while. Tankless units are happier on a service schedule than left alone until they quit.

We fit Navien and Noritz units regularly. More on tankless water heaters.

Air ducts and dryer vents

Supply and return duct cleaning for houses and commercial units, and dryer vent cleaning. A restricted dryer vent shows up as longer drying times well before it becomes a fire risk. If the dryer has got slow, that's the reason to call.

Commercial

Rooftop package units, make-up air, and the heating plant in strata and light industrial buildings. Much of that work sits close to the shop.
